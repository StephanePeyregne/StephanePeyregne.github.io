We are delighted to announce that our lab has received funding from the <a href="https://www.frm.org/en">Fondation pour la Recherche Médicale</a> for our project, The Evolutionary Rise of Modern Disease Risk.

The project will explore how human evolutionary history shaped present-day susceptibility to complex diseases by combining ancient genomes with large-scale human genomic and medical data. By tracing disease-associated variants across time, from ancient hominins to present-day populations, we hope to better understand why genetic risk for common diseases persists today.

This funding will also support the recruitment of a bioinformatician and a postdoctoral researcher in the lab.
