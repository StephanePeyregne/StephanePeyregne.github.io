Euan is a postdoctoral researcher working at the intersection of evolutionary biology, statistical genetics, and epidemiology. Euan did his PhD in evolutionary medicine at the University of Groningen, supervised by Hannah Dugdale, Virpi Lummaa, and Erik Postma. In his PhD, he used different statistical approaches to large human demographic and genomic datasets to explore the evolutionary processes shaping individual variation to ageing and disease. This included leveraging longitudinal variation across 250 years in Finnish demographic data to reveal the connection between human reproductive behaviour and lifespans, and using contemporary genomic and pathology data to explore the role of evolutionary trade-offs shaping genetic predispositions to breast cancer.

Euan is now exploring these questions across a broader spectrum of disease and deeper evolutionary timescales. To this end, he is developing robust methods that combine contemporary and paleogenomic data to assess when genetic variants associated with disease arose and disentangle the role of different evolutionary processes shaping their persistence to the present day.
