Inês completed her MSc in Bioinformatics and Computational Biology at the NOVA School of Science and Technology in Lisbon. For her thesis, she benchmarked computational algorithms for enhanced drug discovery. She then worked as a Bioinforatics Associate Consultant at Clarivate in Barcelona, where she contributed to a range of large-scale genomics and multi-omics projects.

Her work now centers on developing robust and reproducible bioinformatics pipelines for the analysis of ancient and modern human genomic data, as well as their integration with GWAS and biobank datasets.
